About the Atom Lightweight Collaborative Robot

Atom is a lightweight collaborative robot designed for flexible production lines and confined-space operations. With its ultra-light body and compact footprint, Atom can be flexibly deployed in various tight workspaces to collaborate safely with human workers, helping enterprises upgrade to flexible and intelligent production.

Ultra-Light Design, Flexible Deployment

Weighing only about 3kg, Atom can be easily installed on desktops, workbenches, production lines, and other confined spaces. Its compact body allows it to work flexibly in areas that traditional industrial robots cannot reach, providing a brand-new automation solution for flexible production lines.

Safe Collaboration, Human-Robot Harmony

Atom adopts advanced collision detection and torque sensing technologies to perceive external contact in real time and respond safely. With drag-to-teach support, operators can directly guide the robot through complex trajectory programming—no professional programming knowledge required—achieving true human-robot harmony.

Flexible Production, Quick Changeover

Atom supports quick gripper change and a wide range of optional end effectors, allowing flexible switching between different tasks. Advanced motion control algorithms ensure a repeatability of ±0.02mm, reliably handling high-precision tasks such as precision assembly, screw fastening, and vision inspection.
