Product Introduction

Renovating a new home, buying new furniture, welcoming a newborn — behind every joy lies an invisible concern: formaldehyde. Ventilation and green plants can only do so much; only professional treatment can solve the problem from the source. The human body only noticeably senses formaldehyde when it exceeds the standard by 2-3 times or more.

Indoor Air Purification Process

  • 1. Initial formaldehyde and TVOC test: On-site testing per national standard GB/T18883-2022; samples of benzene, toluene, xylene and TVOC are taken back to the laboratory, and results are issued using advanced instruments such as gas chromatographs
  • 2. Determine the treatment plan: Based on test data and the actual environmental pollutants, customize the air treatment plan and construction schedule
  • 3. Masking protection: Professionally mask and protect items that are not formaldehyde pollution sources (home appliances, leather, metal, glass) and water-sensitive items
  • 4. Formaldehyde removal by penetrating decomposition: Apply penetrating decomposition to all bare boards, cut edges and nail holes using bio-enzyme rapid formaldehyde removal nano liquid
  • 5. Free formaldehyde treatment: For different pollution sources in the overall environment, apply air catalyst agents in batches by spraying
  • 6. TVOC odor treatment: Neutralize renovation odors on leather goods, boards, fabrics and walls using chitin purified odor remover spray
  • 7. Cleanup: Remove masking films, clean the whole house, and touch up furniture and floors
  • 8. Retest: Perform a formaldehyde self-test retest per national standard GB/T18883-2022

FAQ

Q: How is testing charged?
A: Testing is 200 yuan per point. For ordinary households, 3-4 points are recommended for more comprehensive and accurate data.

Q: How long does one treatment last, and will it rebound?
A: Under normal use, without secondary renovation or adding large amounts of new furniture, the effect is lasting. We decompose pollutants from the source rather than simply masking odors, so there is basically no rebound.

Q: How soon can I move in or start working after treatment?
A: Generally, 24-72 hours of ventilation after treatment is sufficient; the specific time is adjusted according to the on-site pollution level.

Q: What products are used, and are they harmful to the human body?
A: The products use the triple-action formula of bio-enzyme rapid decomposition, air catalyst long-lasting purification and chitin deep formaldehyde removal; naturally extracted, not a strong chemical agent, non-toxic and non-irritating, maternal & infant safe, with no secondary pollution.

Q: Will construction damage furniture, floors or walls?
A: Special neutral agents are used that do not harm paint surfaces, wallpaper, leather or fabric, and will not cause fading, peeling or marks.
