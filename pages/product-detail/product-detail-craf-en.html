Core Technology: Intelligent Compliant Force Control

The CRAF Force-Control Series is Dobot's new generation of collaborative robots with built-in torque sensors. Each joint integrates a high-precision torque sensor that senses torque changes in real time, enabling precise perception and response to external forces.

Unlike traditional position-controlled robots, the CRAF series uses advanced force-control algorithms to automatically adjust its motion trajectory and applied force upon contact with external objects, achieving truly "compliant" operation. This capability is essential in applications with strict contact-force requirements such as precision assembly, polishing and grinding, and smart welding.

Product Features

  • Full-joint torque feedback:High-precision torque sensors built into every joint provide real-time feedback on joint loads
  • Intelligent compliant control:Automatically adjusts posture and force when contacting workpieces, preventing overshoot and damage
  • Drag-to-teach:Complete teaching by manual dragging without programming, greatly shortening deployment time
  • Collision detection:Sensitive collision detection stops the robot immediately upon contact with foreign objects, ensuring personal safety
  • Multiple models:Available in 5kg, 10kg and 20kg payload models to cover diverse application needs
  • Compatible with mainstream tools:Supports grippers, polishing heads, welding torches and other end effectors

Application Scenarios

The intelligent compliant force control of the CRAF series makes it ideal for the following precision operations:

  • Precision assembly:Precise alignment and assembly of electronic components and precision mechanical parts
  • Grinding & polishing:Automated grinding and surface finishing of castings and hardware with stable contact force
  • Smart welding:Automatically adjusts torch position and pressure along the weld seam to ensure welding quality
  • Medical assistance:Precision operation of medical equipment with gentle contact force to ensure patient safety
  • Semiconductor industry:Wafer handling, chip mounting and other scenarios demanding extreme precision and cleanliness

Technical Advantages

Compared with traditional collaborative robots, the CRAF Force-Control Series offers significant advantages:

Higher force accuracy:±0.1N force-control accuracy precisely controls contact force, ideal for force-sensitive operations.

Faster response:A force-control response time under 20ms enables real-time reaction to unexpected events, ensuring operational safety.

Easier deployment:Drag-to-teach allows non-specialists to get started quickly, greatly lowering the barrier to use.

Greater scalability:Supports a wide range of end effectors and peripherals for easy integration into existing production lines.
