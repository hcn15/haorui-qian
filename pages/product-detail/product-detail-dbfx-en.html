About HARVEST·Dibao Anti-Slip System

HARVEST·Dibao Anti-Slip System is a revolutionary floor safety solution based on nano microwave penetration technology. Using a polymer water-based material that penetrates into and onto the surface of tiles through nano-penetration technology, it forms an anti-slip resistance layer invisible to the naked eye. The product is colorless, odorless, non-toxic, harmless and formaldehyde-free — a truly green and eco-friendly product that solves over 99% of floor anti-slip problems, safeguarding walking safety and preventing slip injuries.

Product Introduction

HARVEST·Dibao Anti-Slip System is developed by HARVEST Green Technology (Beijing) and recommended by the Science & Technology Department of the Eurasian Industry Committee. Using nano microwave penetration technology, the polymer water-based material penetrates into the interior and surface of the tile through nano-penetration technology, forming an anti-slip resistance layer invisible to the naked eye. After treatment, the floor appearance remains unchanged and the original gloss and texture are unaffected; the micropores form integrally with the floor for a long-lasting effect. The anti-slip grade reaches Ad or above, meeting national aging-friendly renovation standards.

Anti-Slip Principle

This product works on the principle of nano-penetration technology, achieving a lasting anti-slip effect in three steps:

  • Penetration: the polymer water-based material penetrates into the interior and surface of the tile via nano-penetration technology, forming an anti-slip resistance layer invisible to the naked eye
  • Reaction: organic acids, inorganic acids, additives and fluoride salts form a synergistic system that etches uniform nano-scale micropores of consistent size and depth on the tile surface
  • Resistance: the micropores use the vacuum adsorption principle; upon contact with oil or water they form a physical suction cup with the sole, greatly increasing the friction coefficient
