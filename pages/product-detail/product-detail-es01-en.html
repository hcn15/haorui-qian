Product Overview

DOBOT ES01 is a compact electric vacuum gripper developed by DOBOT specifically for the Magician E6 six-axis collaborative robot. Its integrated design delivers plug-and-play operation with no external air supply required and low operating noise. Combined with the DobotStudio Pro platform, which offers Blockly block-based programming and Lua scripting, it is simple to use and quickly enables automated gripping solutions for education and research, easily meeting teaching needs.

Key Features

Easy Deployment

The integrated design fits the aviation connector at the end of the Magician E6, delivering plug-and-play setup without cumbersome wiring.

Simple to Use

Controlled directly via the DobotStudio Pro platform with easy Blockly block-based programming, while also supporting efficient Lua scripting.

Compact & Practical

Compact in size, requires no external air supply, runs quietly, and easily meets teaching needs.

Flexible Expansion

Suction nozzles support quick attachment and removal, and multiple nozzles can be connected to adapt to more application scenarios.

Application Scenarios

Ideal for teaching simulations of small-part loading and unloading, handling, palletizing, processing, packaging, and other applications.
